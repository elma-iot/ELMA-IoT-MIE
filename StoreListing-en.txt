ELMA-IoT MIE brings equipment records, well schematics and reusable illustrations into one Windows workspace for wellhead equipment teams. Follow hardware from delivery through Inventory, installation, return, inspection and release.

EQUIPMENT TRACEABILITY
Maintain Master Equip. list, Inventory, Quarantine and In-Order views. Separate PN, SN, Location and Comments fields sit under editable equipment sections. Record partial receipts and controlled moves with reasons, dates and user or PC identity. Duplicate tips identify matching wells and offer a retained or permanent transfer. Retained installed equipment stays in its original Inventory position with an orangish whole-row marker and remains unavailable until returned. Inspection and release rules still apply afterward. Permanent transfer removes the Inventory placeholder while keeping history.

SCHEMATICS AND HISTORY
Import readable XLSX or PDF schematics and link them to wells. Review detected values before applying them. Inventory supplies the authoritative PN for a same-serial correction; exact reviewed drawing references identify editable labels, while ambiguous or flattened drawings retain an explicit correction note. Updated drawings offer a choice to keep their PN or use the corrected installed PN. Original external files remain unchanged.
Right-click equipment to display its history timeline. Search by SN, follow locations over time, and open the exact full log record from an event. The illustration library reuses identical resources while preserving placement dimensions and transforms. An optional bundled viewer supports user-supplied static GLB models.

EDIT, PRINT AND EXCHANGE
Global Undo and Redo stay beside Sync and work across tabs. Edit cells and formatting, add rows and fields, find/replace, and manage supported column headings. Print the active tab or save PDF. Excel extraction preserves visible data, styling, exact identifiers, dates and cell Notes; timeline extraction includes the graph and event table. Native project XLSX export retains formulas and linked drawings.
Save compressed MIE projects with shared resources. Background saving, backup and synchronization use snapshots so later edits can remain pending for the next operation. Incremental processing reuses unchanged resources where applicable. Competing sync edits require review; recovery history remains available.

FREE AND OPTIONAL LICENSED FEATURES
Local records, lifecycle workflows and local-folder/network-share synchronization are available in Free. An optional owner-issued license enables the existing branding, cloud synchronization, encryption and planning features under separately agreed terms. Activation does not process payment or start a subscription. Licensed Min. Stock, Metrics and Rig/Well Schedule provide reviewed policies, dated activities and procurement estimates, not approved orders or confirmed designs. Existing encrypted projects still require their correct key.

LANGUAGES AND APPEARANCE
The interface, guide and synthetic tutorials support 20 languages, including right-to-left layouts. Language and guide data download on demand and remain available offline afterward. Imported project data retains its original content and language. Theme Studio covers light, dark and custom themes, graphs, cell highlights and tips. Tutorial demonstrations restore the original project.

PROJECT DATA AND ACTIVATION
Projects stay local unless you choose to synchronize, export or share them. License activation creates an encrypted request with your name, surname, machine identity and request time for you to provide to the owner; licensed features verify internet time. No publisher account or advertising is required.

REQUIREMENTS AND OPTIONAL CONTENT
Windows 10/11 x64 with .NET Framework 4.8. Microsoft Excel is needed for native XLSX schematic import and rendering. Network access is used for optional data downloads and configured synchronization or licensing services. Store executable modules are bundled in the MSIX and are not downloaded as executable updates. Newer project workflows may require a newer application reader; minimum-reader checks protect incompatible records. No customer data, owner keys or project credentials are included.
