ELMA-IoT MIE 1.16.1 — replace linked drawings and clarify cross-tab stock warnings

- Schematics has Replace immediately to the left of Export. Select the well drawing, choose an updated XLSX/PDF and reuse the existing Review schematic import workflow: Keep drawing only, or review destinations/values and Apply import to Master Equip. list.
- Replacement stays tied to the selected document and well even when the source filename/title differs. Existing links remain valid, including an updated extension when changing file type. A uniquely linked well is locked for equipment review. Ambiguous rows require selection.
- The new source is imported and rendered in an isolated copy before committing. Failed imports and picker cancellation retain the old drawing. The original source file is read-only. The drawing replacement and later reviewed equipment edits have separate Undo/Redo steps and revision records. Closing the review keeps the replacement and leaves equipment unchanged.
- Serial warnings check Inventory, installed Master Equip. list, Quarantine and open In-Order serial entries. Each affected record glows; hover cards identify the other locations and explain available-versus-installed, available-versus-quarantined and installed-versus-quarantined conflicts. Automatic Outstanding issues clear after correction. These checks run on committed cell edits and table refresh.
- Repeated PNs with different serials remain valid. Min. Stock policies and schematic mirrors are not additional physical units. Fully received/cancelled orders are excluded; placeholder serials such as N/A, Unknown and Not installed do not produce duplicate warnings.
- Normal installation/quarantine transfers retain their existing atomic behavior: Inventory is cleared and both endpoints support Undo/Redo. Cross-tab warnings also work in separated stock field views and when a previously hidden tab is activated.
- On-demand language/help packs cover all 20 languages and 57 guide topics, including a safe Replace demonstration and a cross-tab duplicate/correction demonstration. Core EXE remains small.

Compatibility: Existing project formats, license keys and previous 1.16.0 merge/sharing features remain supported. XLSX drawing import uses installed Excel; saved drawings can be viewed without it. No operational well data, keys or credentials are included. Store submission is not part of this review release.

Run MIE Portable.exe on Windows 10/11 with .NET Framework 4.8. Extract the ZIP first. Keep your existing project and settings folders. Language/help content downloads on demand and remains available offline after download.
