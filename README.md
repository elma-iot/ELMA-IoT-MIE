# ELMA-IoT MIE

**Subsea wellhead equipment management from purchase order through final disposal**

Bring equipment records, technical documents, well schematics, and 3D facility views into one Windows workspace.

[Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NSZMSQKXH67) · [What's new in 1.19.69](ReleaseNotes-1.19.69.md) · [User guide](UserGuide.txt)

</div>

## What teams can do

- **Track the same item across teams.** Procurement, warehouse, engineering, and operations can follow a serial from receipt and storage through installation, inspection, and disposal. Duplicate warnings help keep the register consistent.
- **Find equipment in the workshop.** Named 3D locations connect Inventory records to equipment on the shelf. Open a location from Inventory or find an item's record from the 3D scene.
- **Keep handovers digital.** Link schematics, manuals, inspection reports, and approvals to equipment records and carry their history into evidence reports.
- **Work with 2D and 3D well information.** View linked drawings, build well assemblies, and review sections and dimensions.
