# ELMA-IoT MIE 1.19.69

Highlights since 1.19.56.

- **Storage.** Import a GLB facility, mark named locations, and move or rotate textured pallets. Multiple items can share a pallet when they fit. The picker shows descriptions and sorts eligible items by weight and serial number.
- **Inventory navigation.** Click **3D Map Location** to fly to the exact item. Hover over it for its part number, serial number, and description, or choose **See in Inventory** to highlight its row.
- **Equipment lifecycle.** Filter Inventory by location and status. The licensed Disposal register retains history and evidence, supports documented reversals, and exports PDF and Excel reports with serial-specific model views.
- **Technical documents.** Find part-number references in indexed manuals, open the matching PDF page, and use sourced descriptions and weights in Inventory. PDF viewing adds continuous scrolling, search, and clickable contents links.
- **3D engineering.** Serial-specific notes, marks, and damage follow equipment across Inventory, Quarantine, and Disposal. Geological section previews show progress. Storage adds HDRI, interior reflections, animated water, and hardware usage indicators. Direct3D 12 and the WPF fallback remain available.
- **Workspace.** Improved startup and incremental saves, and added a language selector and role-based walkthroughs to the welcome tour.

The x64 package was prepared for Microsoft Store submission. Check the listing for availability. [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NSZMSQKXH67)
