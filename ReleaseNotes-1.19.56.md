# MIE 1.19.56

- Detach the Well Construction table in 3D Demo or the Well Constructor Wizard. The table can stay open while you check other application tabs.
- Enter lithology and Planned and Actual surveys from the table. Download XLSX templates, review flagged intervals or stations, and save draft data for later use. Valid lithology intervals drive the formation appearance in Cut View.
- Add absolute depth leaders or relative dimensions to DATUM. Drag labels to reposition them and use the alignment guide to line them up. The DATUM leader supports an editable reported depth and caption.
- Cut View closes recoverable GLB seams and keeps the bore visible. Right-click metal cut faces to choose a color and adjust hatch spacing, line thickness and angle. The project saves those choices per part number.
- Connected casing joints share their lead and tail cement sequence. Edits to equipment travel and Top Fill reuse unaffected geometry.
- Expanded help and wizard tutorial captions, with translations in all 20 supported languages.
