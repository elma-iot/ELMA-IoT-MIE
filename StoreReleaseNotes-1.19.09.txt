Version 1.19.09
New licensed Item View brings equipment status, retained inspection documents and original GLB models together. Fit, zoom and pan images; review evidence through the existing inspection/release workflow.
The bundled 3D viewer adds textures and mapping controls, perspective/orthographic views, movable X/Y/Z sections, surface damage marking and reversible deformation with Undo/Redo. Live brush previews show placement; paint is independent of model textures. Left click raises, right click dents and middle drag pans. A soft circular ground provides separate shadow and optional model reflection.
Damage checkpoints save asynchronously when editing finishes, without the global busy indicator. Original models and historical damage records are retained; returning equipment clears active damage.
Updated guides, tutorials and all 20 language catalogs, plus interface, dialog-focus and rendering refinements.
