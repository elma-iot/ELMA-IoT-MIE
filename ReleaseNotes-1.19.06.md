# MIE 1.19.06 — optional 3D viewer tools

Portable review and local Microsoft Store candidate. Upload and Store submission remain on hold pending owner testing. Version 2.0 remains deferred.

- The optional native viewer retains the hardware-capable WPF/Direct3D path, using the adapter selected by Windows and the graphics driver. The settings panel reports the rendering tier. Normal orbiting avoids automatic per-triangle mouse hit tests; picking occurs for zoom and painting. View-cube labels are frozen bitmaps, texture projections reuse frozen geometry and bounded caches, camera damping settles quickly, and idle/hidden scenes stop their render timers. Automatic rotation defaults to off. Preference writes are batched in the background. The verification machine reports rendering tier 2; Intel, AMD and NVIDIA are supported through the same vendor-independent Windows path, rather than separate renderers.
- Section axis and position remain selectable while Cut view is off. Enabling it uses the prepared values. Camera position, material/color, textures, placement, lighting, section settings and brush controls are remembered separately for each item/model. Initial viewport sizing preserves a saved camera distance. The model coordinate system remains consistent in right-to-left interface languages.
- Wheel zoom preserves the surface point under the pointer, with a target-plane fallback over empty space. Navigate mode uses left drag to orbit and right/middle drag to pan. Pallet boards now have face UVs and procedural wood grain, including theme tinting.
- Added 14 original procedural texture presets covering wood, brushed metal, concrete, stone, brick, tiles, fabric, leather, rubber, plastic, glass, ground, wall paint and paper. Upload PNG/JPEG textures of up to 16 MB; preview decoding is bounded and originals are retained with the equipment. Choose original UV, box, planar X/Y/Z, cylindrical or spherical placement, plus repeat/mirror/clamp wrapping, U/V repeat and offsets, and rotation. These are native material previews and original swatches, rather than redistributed V-Ray library assets.
- Added surface-conforming damage marking. Spot defaults to reddish with size/softness controls; Circle defaults to white with size/thickness controls. Colors are selectable. Marks use the model surface and scene lighting, remain attached across deformation, and respect section clipping.
- Raise and Lower brushes edit a preview deformation layer using size, softness and strength. Holding the brush builds the effect; coarse surfaces refine locally within bounded limits. Computation runs in the background. The original GLB bytes remain unchanged. Deformation is a visual record, rather than a dimensional inspection measurement.
- Viewer Undo/Redo handles complete brush gestures, deformations, material/texture edits and Reset model. It supports Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z, including the large viewer. History is bounded to 64 local states. The main Undo/Redo controls prioritize the active viewer. Reset model restores original materials and geometry and clears active damage; reset is undoable.
- Damage saves automatically after interaction, with an explicit Save damage marks retry. Working marks are retained on failed saves; concurrent damage changes require review and Reload saved damage confirms discarding unsaved work. Switching documents, saving or leaving a project flushes pending edits. Damage checkpoints carry user/PC history and are retained as immutable deduplicated resources through native save, incremental save, backup and sync. New annotation-bearing projects require MIE 1.19.06 or newer.
- Return to Inventory and approved release clear active decals/deformations while preserving prior checkpoints and selected material, color, texture and scene parameters. Workbook Undo/Redo restores the matching damage state. Internal checkpoints are omitted from the normal attachment list; history retains the related checkpoint. Original uploaded textures remain ordinary downloadable attachments.
- All rendering, texture generation/mapping, brush, deformation and viewer-history code stays in the verified optional 3D pack. The main application supplies loading, storage, audit history and command integration. The Store candidate bundles the approved pack and matching Theme Studio; portable installations download once or use Browse pack offline. The guide, tutorial content and new labels are updated in all 20 languages.

Validation covers pointer zoom, selectable inactive section controls, GPU-eligible rendering, textured pallet UVs, spot/circle defaults and surface hits, undo/redo/reset, coarse-mesh Raise/Lower refinement, dispatcher responsiveness, damage/section clipping, retained original geometry, clearing on return, historical bytes after native save/reopen, inspection restrictions and Item View PDF/Excel output. Native rotatable Excel objects retain the original GLB; the printable snapshot records the displayed view. The validated Store candidate is held privately pending owner testing and authorization.
