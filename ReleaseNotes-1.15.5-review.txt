ELMA-IoT MIE 1.15.5 - Quarantine and automatic Autofill review

Quarantine (free edition)
- New tab immediately after Inventory and before In-Order, with equipment sections, normal editing/formatting, custom fields, search, zoom and detach/attach.
- Damaged / Scrap dropdown: awaiting inspection or unfit for use. Both are excluded from available Inventory and installation matching.
- Inventory Edit > select an item > Quarantine transfers its PN/SN, location, notes and additional fields. Select target section, status, actual movement date and inspection reference. Source stock is cleared and a Transfer movement is recorded.
- Empty Quarantine cells can use existing matching-stock selection with status choice. Existing destinations are never silently overwritten.
- One Undo/Redo restores/repeats the entire movement. Native project storage, revisions and three-way sync retain it.
- Free Excel export includes Quarantine sections and a Damaged/Scrap validation dropdown. Excel reimport retains status.
- Projects containing quarantined items require MIE 1.15.5 or newer. Older project files remain readable in this version. Existing license identity and keys are unchanged.

Minimum-stock Autofill (licensed)
- Table/sparkle icon immediately before Edit, including detached Min. Stock windows.
- Opens and analyzes eligible historical wells and the active rig programme automatically. Historical-well selection is an optional override.
- Disabled with a hover explanation unless there is a valid approved dated PN requirement, or an upcoming drilling activity with qualifying history: three completed wells, three positive PN examples and 30-day span.
- Historical PN variants use their positive-record share within each equipment slot. Unknown slots are excluded; variants are not all assumed on every future well. Explicit region restricts the cohort; pooled/unspecified designs carry lower-confidence evidence.
- Approved requirements take precedence per activity. Estimates require review, default unchecked, retain higher manual minima, and apply as one undoable batch. Unknown lead time remains unfilled.
- This is a dated schedule/install-mix estimator, not a learned continuous demand or shortage-probability model. Policy movement forecasting has separate requirements.

Help and distribution
