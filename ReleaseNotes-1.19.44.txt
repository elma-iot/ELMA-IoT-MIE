What's new in 1.19.44

• Operate configured valves directly in 3D Demo and the Well Constructor Wizard. Connected LP wellhead valves show underwater bubbles, and handle changes update the affected meshes in place.
• Author and edit threaded casing, tubing and drill pipe in Item View. Set Bottom Extend depths and enter cement tail and lead depths for cut views.
• Set a default 3D camera per well. Cut view keeps your camera position and fades measurements back in when navigation stops.
• The Well Constructor Wizard restores its saved monitor and window layout. The user guide and tutorials cover the new tools.
Portable edition also includes the interactive MIE Well screensaver with valve and slope indicator controls, corrosion-cap lift and return, and configurable music.
